 The part of the renderer UMBRA depends on: the directional shadow map and the test against it.
 Everything the player does about health goes through one function: the lit fraction from sunprobe.frag, which is
 the same comparison as shader.frag:   light-space depth of the point  -  bias  >  stored depth  =>  in shadow.

 WHAT GOES WRONG IN THE GAME WHEN EACH PART IS WRONG
   bias too small           acne: sunlit ground falsely shaded in stripes -> sunlit fraction flickers -> damage flickers
                            while standing still; players stand in "safe" stripes that are not shade.
   bias too large           peter-panning: a lit strip beside every wall -> damage in a place that looks like solid shade.
   box too small / off      outside the box = lit: edges of the level burn you for no visible reason.
   box too large            texel size grows (2h/res): shade edges blur by up to one texel, acne threshold rises.
   box follows w/o snapping every shadow edge re-quantises each frame: the safe/lethal boundary shimmers by one texel.
   shadows off (key 0)      nothing blocks the sun: the level is unwinnable (proof the shadows are not decoration).
   wrong matrix/near/far    shadows land in the wrong place; the game and the picture agree with each other but not with the level.
   no texel snapping + follow  see above.
   sun probe a frame late   (documented decision) the damage lags the picture by ~16 ms: invisible, and it is the same lag for everyone.
